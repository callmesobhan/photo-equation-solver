## How to request a commercial licence

1. **Open a licence request:**
   [new licence request](https://github.com/callmesobhan/photo-equation-solver/issues/new?template=licence-request.yml) (or contact `@callmesobhan` on GitHub).
2. **Tell the author** what you want to use it for, which edition (Pocket, Local, Agent/MCP, CLI, or Teacher Kit), how many users/devices, and expected duration.
3. **Do not post private details** (email, phone, payment details) in a public issue; the author will arrange a private channel once you open the request.
4. **A commercial licence starts when the author confirms it in writing.**
